## Not pursued, with reasons
- **"copier trade" (2400/mo, KD 26–68 depending on report), "trade copier" (1600–1900, KD 50–56), "copy trading platforms":** generic or mixed-intent terms (forex and crypto copy platforms), dominated by tradesyncer.com and tradecopia.com home pages. The engine scores them low.
- **"how to copy trade on tradingview" (140, KD 9), "… lucid trading" (70), "… polymarket" (140):** TradeLink has no published TradingView, Lucid or Polymarket capability, so a page would either invent one or be off-topic.
- **"apex trade copier" (260, KD 12):** `/apex-tradovate-trade-copier/` already exists and passes the gate. Wait for Search Console data before changing it.
- **"tradesyncer" (18,100) and "tradecopia" (9,900):** navigational brand searches; the engine never builds pages for them. The comparison pages already exist (see item 5).
- **Outdated pages:** none yet. Every page was modified on 2026-10-16, so the 120-day outdated rule doesn't apply.

## Caveats
- The proposals edit pages that changed today. The engine's 28-day cooling-off normally prevents this. It is acceptable here only because no page has a measured baseline yet (no GSC rows, no Semrush rankings).
- Volumes differ between Semrush reports for the same keyword (for example "trade copier" 1600 vs 1900), and the snapshot records both. This document quotes `phrase_these`/`phrase_questions` values.
- CPC currency is not stated in the responses, so CPC is not used here.
