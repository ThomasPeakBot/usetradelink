Why the $40 versus $49.99 comparison can mislead

TradeLink's $40 plan is already server-side. Tradecopia's $49.99 Pro plan is local desktop software, which is not exactly the same hosting model. A trader who wants copying to continue without leaving a computer running should compare TradeLink's $40 plan with Tradecopia's hosted Pro+ Lite at its advertised $79.99 monthly price.

Tradecopia may offer quarterly or annual discounts and first-month promotions that lower effective costs, so use its current pricing at checkout rather than treating a single figure as permanent. The right comparison includes any operating requirements, integration constraints, and how often you need to access the copier away from your desk.

Where Tradecopia wins: broader platform coverage and unlimited followers

Tradecopia advertises direct integrations with Tradovate, NinjaTrader, Rithmic and TopstepX, plus TradingView-based routes, whereas TradeLink currently supports accounts through Tradovate. Tradecopia also states unlimited followers on its plans, versus TradeLink's advertised 150-account maximum.

If your portfolio mixes Tradovate and Rithmic, or if 150 accounts is not enough, that may matter more than price. Do not buy TradeLink for an unsupported platform because an integration was discussed or described as coming soon. See prop firm policies, which are separate from software connectivity.

Where TradeLink fits: one price and Tradovate-first simplicity

For traders whose current accounts are all reachable through supported Tradovate logins, TradeLink's flat $40 price and server-side operation are its clearest differentiators. Its advertised plan includes up to 150 accounts, unlimited supported logins and groups, per-follower sizing and risk tools. You do not need to upgrade simply because you add another supported Tradovate login within the account limit.

Tradecopia's richer platform list can justify its higher price for some users. But when those additional platforms are unnecessary, paying for them may not improve your workflow. Focus on the actual connection list, how you manage followers and what support response is available when an order fails.

Choosing the correct product for your setup

TradeLink may suit you if…

Every account uses a supported Tradovate connection, you have no more than 150 accounts and you want a cloud copier at one advertised monthly price.

Tradecopia may suit you if…

You need multiple platform types, an unlimited follower allowance, a local desktop option, or its particular reporting and reconciliation tools.

Neither tool guarantees exact execution synchronization. Verify partial fills, rejection reporting, margin, drawdown policies and all firm-specific restrictions before going live.

How to evaluate switching without double-copying

  1. Record your leader, followers, broker logins and any unsupported connections.
  2. Compare total cost using the hosting tier you will actually run.
  3. Confirm your prop firm's current copying policy for your own accounts.
  4. Disable copying from the old service before running the new copying group.
  5. Test a small supervised trade and inspect broker-side positions and risk controls.
  6. Only then migrate additional groups and cancel the old subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Is TradeLink cheaper than Tradecopia?

At published monthly list prices, TradeLink is $40. Tradecopia Pro Desktop is $49.99 and its web-hosted Pro+ Lite is $79.99. Billing promotions and the different hosting models matter.

Does Tradecopia offer a free trial?

Tradecopia lists a seven-day trial for Pro Desktop and Pro+ Lite with a payment method required. Its dedicated Pro+ plan does not include that trial.

Can TradeLink copy Rithmic accounts?

Not at present according to TradeLink's public compatibility information. Rithmic is marked coming soon; Tradecopia advertises Rithmic support.

Does Tradecopia allow more than 150 follower accounts?

Tradecopia publishes an unlimited follower allowance. TradeLink advertises up to 150 accounts per plan. Firm account limits still apply.
