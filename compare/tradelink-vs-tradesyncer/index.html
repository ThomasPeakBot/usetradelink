How much could you pay for a futures copier?

TradeLink advertises one $40/month plan with support for up to 150 accounts and unlimited supported connections. TradeSyncer currently lists three monthly tiers: $49, $99 and $149. Its Basic plan has two connections and up to ten accounts per connection. Pro has four connections and up to twenty accounts per connection. Flex includes unlimited connections and 120 accounts, with expansion available. See TradeSyncer's current pricing.

The practical difference is not just the sticker price: count your distinct broker logins, accounts and target trading platforms. A platform with lower listed account capacity may still be right for you if it has the particular integration or reporting feature you need.

Where TradeSyncer currently has an advantage

TradeSyncer publishes broader platform coverage, including Rithmic and other provider integrations. As of this page update, TradeLink's own site lists Tradovate and NinjaTrader, with Rithmic coming soon. If your account requires Rithmic or another currently unsupported connection, do not switch on the assumption that a future integration is live.

TradeSyncer also markets a more extensive built-in reporting, journaling and analytics suite. TradeLink advertises journaling and CSV export, but those claims alone do not establish feature parity. We recommend checking the workflows you actually use before making a decision.

Who should choose TradeLink—and who should choose TradeSyncer?

Choose TradeLink if…

You already use a supported futures connection and want a clear $40/month price, a large account allowance, group-based copying and risk controls without paying for higher account-count tiers.

Consider TradeSyncer if…

You require a platform such as Rithmic today, prefer its current reporting and journaling offerings, or want to evaluate its features through the published seven-day trial before choosing a paid plan.

In either case, verify whether copying is allowed under your specific broker and prop firm's current policies. Futures trade copying does not eliminate slippage, rejected orders, account mismatches or loss risk.

Frequently asked questions

Is TradeLink cheaper than TradeSyncer?

At currently advertised monthly prices, yes: TradeLink lists $40/month while TradeSyncer's Basic starts at $49/month. Their included connections, accounts and features differ, so compare your entire workflow.

Can TradeLink copy trades across 150 accounts?

TradeLink advertises support for up to 150 accounts on one subscription, subject to supported connections, broker access and normal order and risk limitations. This is a capacity claim, not a guarantee of identical fills.

Does TradeLink support Rithmic?

The TradeLink homepage currently marks Rithmic as coming soon, not live. Verify supported brokers before you subscribe.

Does TradeSyncer require a VPS?

TradeSyncer markets a cloud-based trade copier with no VPS required. TradeLink also advertises server-side operation without needing your browser open.

Can I switch from TradeSyncer to TradeLink?

Yes, if your trading accounts are supported by TradeLink. Set up and validate a small test configuration first, then confirm that your leader/follower rules, fills and risk settings behave as expected. TradeLink advertises a switching promotion; see its current eligibility and proof requirements on the homepage.

Ready to compare your setup with TradeLink?

Check your broker compatibility, account count and risk configuration before migrating. If TradeLink fits, its advertised plan is $40/month.
