Which brokers are supported?

TradeLink currently supports NinjaTrader and Tradovate, with Rithmic coming soon. The experience is built specifically for futures copy trading and prop-trader workflows.

Can I use live and demo accounts?

Yes. TradeLink is designed for common futures-prop workflows involving evals, PAs, funded accounts, and testing environments, all visible inside the same operating model.

Does my browser need to stay open?

No. The copier is intended to run server-side. The app acts as your control center for setup, monitoring, activity, and protection rules.
