Tradovate trade copier: how to copy trades across Tradovate accounts

Does Tradovate have a trade copier? Partly. Tradovate's built-in Group Trade sends one order to several accounts from the same provider, with a quantity you set for each account. It can't group accounts at different prop firms, and it doesn't copy fills. To trade once and have followers at several firms mirror your leader account's fills, you need a cloud trade copier such as TradeLink, which connects to Tradovate for $40/month.

How Tradovate Group Trade works

Tradovate's help article describes Group Trade as a way to place one order across a group of accounts:

  • Create groups from the account dropdown in the Tradovate web or desktop app with Manage Groups. The option only appears when your login has more than one account.
  • Set a quantity per account. The first account you add becomes the group's lead account.
  • Order sizes must be multiples of the group total. With three accounts set to 1 contract each, you can only send orders in multiples of 3.
  • Same provider only. Accounts from the same prop firm provider can be grouped. You can't combine, for example, a Tradovate brokerage account with a prop firm evaluation account.
  • Other limits: ATM strategies are disabled for groups of more than five accounts, very large group orders can hit API rate limits, and Group Trade isn't available in the mobile app.

For one firm's accounts under one login, Group Trade is often enough, and it's built in. The trouble starts when you trade evaluations or funded accounts at several prop firms, which each give you a separate Tradovate login.

Frequently asked questions

Does Tradovate have a trade copier?

Tradovate has a built-in feature called Group Trade. It sends one order to a group of accounts from the same provider, with a set quantity per account. It isn't a fill-based copier and can't group accounts from different providers, such as two different prop firms. For that you need a third-party trade copier.

Can you copy trade between two prop firms on Tradovate?

Not with Group Trade, which only groups accounts from the same provider. A cloud trade copier such as TradeLink connects each Tradovate login separately and copies the leader account's fills into followers at other firms, if each firm's rules allow copying.

How much does a Tradovate trade copier cost?

Group Trade is part of the Tradovate web and desktop apps. TradeLink costs $40/month for unlimited Tradovate connections and up to 150 accounts. Other third-party copiers usually price by connections or accounts.

Do I need to keep my browser or a VPS running?

Not with TradeLink. It copies server-side, so the web app is only for setup and monitoring. Group Trade is used from inside the Tradovate web or desktop app while you place the order.

Can I use different contract sizes on each follower?

Yes. In TradeLink each follower can copy 1:1, use a multiplier or use a fixed quantity, so an evaluation and a funded account don't have to size alike.
